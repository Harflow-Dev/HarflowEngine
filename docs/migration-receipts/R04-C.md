## Limits and intended guarantees

Coordinator limits:256 total retained sentinel FDs, including failed/raced aliases;
1024 shared leases per sentinel; zero queued acquisition; one nonblocking kernel
lock attempt; Busy on local/coordinator contention. Admission checks use
min(caller,start+2seconds). Successful sentinel descriptors and quarantined candidates
are never evicted/closed during process lifetime. Capacity exhaustion refuses.
This deliberate lifetime cap must be revisited before a long-lived P2 runtime.

Dedicated regular single-link nofollow sentinel ownership, physical path/inode
binding and local reader/writer state protect process-scoped record locks. Final
local lease unlocks without closing the descriptor. Lifetime Store/root claims
remain held across launch; no temporary release for spawning. OS calls are
nonpreemptible; admission checks do not promise hard wall-clock completion.

Safe Darwin posix_spawn uses CLOEXEC_DEFAULT and explicit stdio dup actions;
parent endpoints remain owned, normalized source descriptors avoid destination
collisions. No unsafe/custom fork/pre_exec or fabricated std::Child. Existing
input bounds remain128 arguments/128 environment entries,64KiB combined argv/env,
4096byte paths and1MiB capture per stream. Safe wrapper lacks CWD actions: requested
physical CWD must equal inherited CWD, otherwise refuse before execution. Fresh
fixture bootstrap may establish its validated directory before threads/Store;
general multithreaded chdir is not used. Broader CWD support is a later gate.
Executable path/regular/executable-bit and device/inode bookends detect observed
replacement; they do not establish executed-byte identity against hostile concurrent
replacement or in-place modification. Qualification assumes cooperatively managed
executables/paths, not an OS security boundary. A changed post-spawn bookend retains
the child and refuses signal authority rather than returning non-execution.

Actual child identity and sole wait authority are retained; late spawn results do
not become non-execution. Unexpected external reaping makes authority unknown and
refuses later signals. Exit observation, capture, drain and containment remain
separate; no descendant/control guarantee is added.
