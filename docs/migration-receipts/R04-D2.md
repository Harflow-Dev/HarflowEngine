## Delivered mechanics

See [output-drain design](../owner-output-drain.md) for exact finite bounds and
[D3 lifecycle qualification](R04-D3.md) for the consolidated production gates.
The original main thread retains OwnerSession<Store>, root lease, raw files and
original child/sole waiter. Exactly one private reader owns the two moved output
FDs after exact EffectDone and before acceptance persistence. Qualified empty-state
handoff is one-time. Failed thread startup after transfer explicitly loses pipe
capture; cleanup preserves the original child and uncertainty rather than retrying.

A data-only4-slot queue carries at most16384 bytes per stream per batch. First
Full/disconnected send permanently discards that batch and all later bytes while
continuing finite reads. Exact per-stream read/enqueued/discarded counts and immutable
EOF/error/final observations remain available independently of the FIFO. No spool,
storage worker, new sentinel FD, duplicated pipe, eviction or cap increase is added.
Maximum logical payload is196608 bytes including producer/consumer in-flight batches;
this is not a measured RSS bound.

A healthy writer persists the finite accepted contiguous prefix, then records known
queue loss. A raw/index/gate error poisons immediately; no later storage mutation
occurs. Accepted bytes are separately acknowledged, abandoned after poison, or
uncertain after attempted raw/index mutation. Uncertain bytes are not declared
durable or exactly lost. Raw append→sync→qualified index/event→ack order remains.
A poisoned acceptance path has no fabricated acceptance fence or capture grant.

EOF/terminal requires immutable final, joined reader, reconciled empty FIFO, no
loss/error/stop, original-child exit and closed input. A volatile Code(0)/bothEOF
may coexist with persisted Unobserved exit after poison/loss. No reservation is
released and replay still observes the original generation/effect.

The private OwnerChild guard settles reader and original child on success/error/
unwind before Store/root teardown. It never detaches a live worker or joins an
unfinished one without a bounded finished check. Unresolved cleanup aborts/exits
the private owner before Store unwind. Original wait authority, including permanent
ECHILD loss, is retained. General platform/process Drop policy is unchanged.

## Failure and qualification limits

Reader bounds are8MiB per stream,4096 rounds/8192 read attempts,≤20ms poll and the
earlier of original deadline/start+8s. Limits are incomplete/unknown unread tail,
not synthetic EOF. Owner8s/effect4s/dispatcher10s are fixed fixture budgets; observer
lifetime does not reset them. Separate private cleanup budgets are documented in D3.

Main-thread storage calls remain nonpreemptible. If one never returns, owner shutdown
is not bounded and Store/root ownership remains held. A later process-isolated
storage proposal requires reviewed identity-bound IPC/ack, commit uncertainty and
lease/crash design; even killing cannot guarantee bounded uninterruptible-I/O death.
No such process boundary, central service or automatic restart is implemented.

Injected finite gates/errors are not actual ENOSPC, failed OS sync, device or power
qualification. No privileged fault setup or host-volume exhaustion occurs. Platform
and filesystem evidence remains local macOS arm64/APFS; no backend, production
budget, containment or full P2 acceptance follows.
