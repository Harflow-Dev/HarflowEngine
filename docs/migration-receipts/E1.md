## Current implementation boundary

`harflow-ctl` supplies `version-info` and lease-free, SQLite-free `state-inspect`.
On macOS arm64, store-backed reads are `list`, `status` (including `--brief`),
`status-batch`, `task-list-page` (including successors), `task-events`,
`task-prompt`, `task-artifacts`, `recovery-activity`, `interaction-events` and
`statistics-page`. Source references: `crates/hf-host/src/ctl/services.rs`,
`crates/hf-host/src/ctl/store_reads.rs` and
`crates/hf-domain/src/ctl/identity.rs`.

Reads classify the root before store opening and use one `ReadSession` snapshot
under a shared lease. `task-events`, `task-prompt` and `statistics-page` check
caller markers and may refuse before root classification. Reads do not initialize
a missing root. The read session
uses no private copy, `io.lock` or per-call `quick_check`, has no store-size cap
of its own, and may create/rewrite `-shm`. Missing/nonregular `-wal` refuses;
the check/open race remains acknowledged. The 2s lease and 5s read deadlines
bound admission/answer acceptance, not hard wall-clock preemption. Caller
admission as `NoAgentDetected` is not a human credential or managed-ownership
qualification. See [read path](../store-read-path.md) and
[storage protocol](../storage-protocol.md).

Fresh roots use schema6 with typed task/process/prompt/artifact facts beside
the existing task/event/watch tables; header/status blobs remain the served
bytes. Schema5 reads refuse as `unsupported_layout`, with no migration.
Open/recover/probe and the separate internal private-copy `RecoveryReadSession`
retain the 64MiB cap. Watch command/capability remains unavailable until E2.
